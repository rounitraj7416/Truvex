The Five-Layer Trust Engine

No single check is sufficient. TRUVEX evaluates every communication across five independent cryptographically bound boundaries.

🏢

1. Business Verification

Weight: 20%

Enterprise accreditation and manual vetting by platform superadministrators. Disqualifies illegitimate companies and assigns sequential Company IDs (e.g. TRX-C-0001).

🪪

2. Employee Directory Check

Weight: 20%

Real-time corporate directory binding. Verifies that the representative is actively employed, assigned an authorized Employee ID (e.g. TRX-E-0001), and has not been suspended.

🛡️

3. Voice Authenticity

Aletheia (20%)

Evaluates pre-call fresh audio through DetectifAI Aletheia (/v1/deepfake/detect). Detects synthetic voice cloning, neural vocoders, and AI-generated speech. Zero tolerance for deepfakes.

🎙️

4. Speaker Biometrics

Eidos (20%)

Voiceprint verification through DetectifAI Eidos (/v1/speakers/verify). Compares acoustic embeddings against the employee's enrolled biometric profile to confirm biological identity.

🔐

5. Ephemeral Session & Challenge

Weight: 20%

Issues a single-use Session ID (10-min TTL) bound to the intended customer and communication topic, accompanied by an interactive 4-digit out-of-band mutual challenge PIN.

⚡

Out-of-Band B2B2C

No Carrier Risk

Bypasses vulnerable SS7/telecom caller-ID spoofing. Customers independently verify any call on the web with zero mobile app downloads or carrier integrations required.
