How TRUVEX Protects You

Traditional caller IDs can be easily spoofed. TRUVEX creates cryptographic proof between organizations and recipients.

1. Vetted Business Registry

Enterprises and authorized representative IDs are accredited and audited by platform security authorities before calls are permitted.

2. Voice Biometric Verification

Representative voice samples are checked for deepfakes and acoustic identity matching before an outbound session is issued.

3. Ephemeral Trust ID

A temporary, 10-minute verifiable code binds the call topic and identity so customers can confirm authenticity without sharing passwords.
